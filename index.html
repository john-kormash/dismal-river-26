Know Before You Go

The Sandhills don't do favors

A few things worth sorting out before we point the cars east on Thursday.

  • 36 a day, two days straight. Whatever shoes you bring, make sure you can walk in them Saturday afternoon.
  • Pick a tee and commit. The White tees on the Red still rate 69.9/128, and the middles on the White rate 71.6/139 — there's no shame in playing up when you're going 36 a day.
  • Wind is the defense. Open sand country with almost no trees — bring a layer and a windshirt even in August.
  • Bring cash. No caddies on this trip — but you'll still want it for tips, the card room, and whatever the matches cost you.
  • Service is thin. Expect patchy cell coverage north of Ogallala. Download directions and the playlist before you leave town.
  • Gas up in Ogallala. It's 95 miles of two-lane to the club with not much in between.
  • Confirm the details. Dress code and club transport should be confirmed with Dismal River before we go.
